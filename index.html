WordPress / Website Redesign and Development

Children's Research Triangle

childrensresearchtriangle.org

Kingdom Branding
DurationJan to Aug 2024
MethodologyHybrid
Team4 people
RoleProject Manager

Children's Research Triangle needed a complete digital overhaul. Their existing website no longer reflected the depth of their clinical services, research programs, or the communities they serve. As Project Manager, I led the engagement from contract through go-live, coordinating a cross-functional team of four across five sequential phases spanning eight months. I opened the project with a discovery session to align the client and internal team on strategic objectives, user goals, and success criteria. From there, I managed two iterative wireframe phases with structured review and approval cycles, established a staging environment for pre-launch quality control, and oversaw inner page development with a defined content population and QA process. Every phase was tracked against a milestone register with assigned owners and approval statuses. The result was a fully responsive, client-approved WordPress site launched on schedule in August 2024, with improved navigation, stronger alignment to CRT's brand and mission, and positive client feedback on communication and delivery quality.

Internal / Process Creation and Optimization

Kingdom Branding Operations

Kingdom Branding

Kingdom Branding Internal
DurationOct to Nov 2024
MethodologyPredictive
Team4 people
RoleProject Manager

Kingdom Branding lacked standardized processes, templates, and documentation to support consistent delivery across departments. Manual tracking, inconsistent pricing communication, and the absence of reusable project frameworks were creating friction and operational inefficiency. As Project Manager, I led an internal improvement initiative over two months to address these gaps systematically. I began with a project charter that defined scope, objectives, and measurable acceptance criteria, then built a work breakdown structure across five major deliverable areas. I developed a stakeholder register, risk register, and assumptions log to govern the project, and implemented change and configuration management plans to maintain version control throughout. I delegated tasks to three junior team members, monitored progress through a structured tracker, and conducted regular CEO reviews to validate outputs against quality criteria. By the close of the project, all milestones had been delivered and approved: a domain tracking dashboard in Looker Studio, updated pricing and proposal sheets, standardized SOP templates, and a reusable project management template library.
