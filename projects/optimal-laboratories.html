The challenge

Optimal Laboratories needed to expand its Shopify store with new product lines but lacked a structured process for doing so accurately and consistently at scale.

My role

  • Owned the project end to end as Project Manager.
  • Coordinated a team of six: a wireframe designer, primary developer, specialist developer for custom coding, QC reviewer, and go-live specialist.
  • Led the discovery and strategy phase.
  • Managed client feedback loops to keep the project on schedule without compromising quality.

Approach

  1. Discovery and strategyGathered product requirements and defined client expectations.
  2. Parallel workstreamsManaged product uploads, description writing, image optimization, and layout adjustments in parallel.
  3. Internal QAReviewed all product pages for accuracy and UX consistency before client-facing review.
  4. Structured approvalsRan a defined approval process at each stage, with managed client feedback loops.
  5. Go-live verificationVerified the launch before closing the project.

Deliverables managed

  • New product lines accurately categorized and live
  • Enhanced product page layouts
  • Documented workflows for future catalog updates

Outcome

The store launched with all new products accurately categorized and live, enhanced product page layouts, and documented workflows that make future catalog updates repeatable.
