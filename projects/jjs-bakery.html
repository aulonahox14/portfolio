Case Study on Kingdom Branding

A monthly social media retainer with on-demand design support for JJ’s Bakery. As Program Manager, I worked alongside the project manager on onboarding, monthly client meetings, data tracking, and a change control process that priced every request outside the retainer.

Client context

Kingdom Branding’s published case study describes a snack pie brand founded in 1986, with strong offline success but a sporadic social presence that lacked a consistent voice. The team built a tailored strategy around high-quality visuals, storytelling, and regular posting, used analytics to see which content resonated, and worked with the brand’s team on more authentic content, including user-generated content.

The case study reports rising engagement and steady follower growth, with social activity driving traffic to the physical location and online orders.

Team and structure

The account team of four to five included an account manager, designers, and content writers, with a project manager leading day-to-day delivery. Social media ran on a monthly retainer, while design support was available on request for work beyond the monthly scope.

My role

  • Worked alongside the project manager as Program Manager.
  • Supported client onboarding.
  • Took part in the monthly client meetings.
  • Tracked data and follow-through so each month’s work stayed aligned with client expectations.
  • Ran change control for every request outside the retainer.

Change control process

  1. IdentifyFlag any request that falls outside the monthly retainer.
  2. DocumentRecord the request as a new service so it can be priced accordingly.
  3. ApproveSend it to the client for approval on a separate purchase order.
  4. Proceed and invoiceBegin work only after approval, then invoice for the added service.

Outcome

The client loved working with the team. The structure kept the retainer scope clear and gave on-demand design requests a defined path to approval.
