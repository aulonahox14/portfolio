A complete digital overhaul for a nonprofit providing research-driven behavioral health services. I led the eight-month, five-phase engagement as Program Manager, from contract through an on-schedule go-live of a responsive, client-approved WordPress website.

8 monthsJan to Aug 2024
5sequential phases
4person cross-functional team
On timeLaunched August 2024

The challenge

Children’s Research Triangle needed a complete digital overhaul. Its existing website no longer reflected the depth of its clinical services, research programs, or the communities it serves.

Kingdom Branding’s published case study adds that the outdated site, fragmented visual identity, and non-responsive design limited the organization’s ability to communicate its mission, engage supporters, and drive donations.

My role

  • Led the engagement from contract through go-live.
  • Coordinated a cross-functional team of four across five sequential phases.
  • Aligned the client and internal team on objectives, scope, and success criteria.
  • Managed review and approval cycles and tracked every phase against a milestone register.

Approach

  1. DiscoveryOpened with a discovery session to align the client and internal team on strategic objectives, user goals, and success criteria.
  2. WireframingManaged two iterative wireframe phases with structured review and approval cycles.
  3. StagingEstablished a staging environment for pre-launch quality control.
  4. Build and QAOversaw inner page development with a defined content population and QA process.
  5. TrackingTracked every phase against a milestone register with assigned owners and approval statuses.

Deliverables managed

  • Fully responsive, client-approved WordPress website
  • Improved navigation and stronger alignment to CRT’s brand and mission

Outcome

The site launched on schedule in August 2024, and the client gave positive feedback on both communication and delivery quality.
