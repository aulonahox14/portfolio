Client context

Kingdom Branding’s published case study describes the scope for the Park District as website design, development, and maintenance, along with print design and merchandise. The goal was a vibrant, user-friendly website and physical branding aligned with the district’s vision.

The challenge

Because it was a very large website, the hardest part was transferring all of the content from the old site to the new one while also meeting the client’s new requests and following the new site structure. That was the key difficulty the team worked through together.

My role

  • Tracked the full website build from start to finish.
  • Created and maintained the project timelines.
  • Managed approvals with the client.
  • Checked in with the team to keep every workstream moving.

Approach

Up to five people worked hands-on: a creative director, designers, and specialists handling hosting and transfer, with others supporting as needed. I coordinated across them through shared timelines and client approval checkpoints, with content migration and structure as the central focus.

Outcome

The website was delivered on time and the client loved it. Tinley Park remains on a monthly maintenance retainer with Kingdom Branding, and we support their website update requests as they come in.
