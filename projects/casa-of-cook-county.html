Client context

CASA of Cook County is the local branch of a national organization that advocates for children in the foster care system, so the website had to tell the local story within the national organization’s design guidelines. Kingdom Branding’s published case study lists a wider scope that also included brand identity, social strategy, and event and fundraising materials.

The homepage led with the Change a Child’s Story campaign, with an events tab directly beneath the hero, and a bank of social graphics, templates, and content helped marathon runners promote their own fundraising.

The challenge

The website work moves at a deliberate pace, so the engagement is an ongoing partnership rather than a fixed-date launch, and it requires steady coordination between the client, the creative lead, and the wider team.

My role

  • Supported the Creative Director, who led the project.
  • Handled the billing side of the engagement.
  • Supported client communication and follow-up.
  • Joined client meetings whenever extra support was needed.

Where it stands

The website engagement is still in progress, with design support provided for events such as the marathons as requests arise.
