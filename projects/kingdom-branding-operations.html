Kingdom Branding Operations

Kingdom Branding  ·  Kingdom Branding Internal

An internal program to standardize how Kingdom Branding delivers work, replacing manual tracking and ad hoc documentation with governed processes, templates, and tools. I led the two-month initiative as Program Manager, and every milestone was delivered and approved.

2 monthsOct to Nov 2024
5major deliverable areas
4person team
Allmilestones delivered and approved

The challenge

Kingdom Branding lacked standardized processes, templates, and documentation to support consistent delivery across departments. Manual tracking, inconsistent pricing communication, and the absence of reusable project frameworks were creating friction and operational inefficiency.

My role

  • Led the initiative end to end as Program Manager, from charter through final approval.
  • Defined scope, objectives, and measurable acceptance criteria with leadership.
  • Delegated work to three junior team members and monitored progress through a structured tracker.
  • Held regular CEO reviews to validate outputs against quality criteria.

Approach

  1. InitiateOpened with a project charter defining scope, objectives, and measurable acceptance criteria.
  2. PlanBuilt a work breakdown structure across five major deliverable areas, supported by a stakeholder register, risk register, and assumptions log.
  3. GovernImplemented change and configuration management plans to maintain version control throughout.
  4. Execute and monitorDelegated tasks across the team and tracked progress in a structured tracker.
  5. ValidateReviewed outputs with the CEO at regular checkpoints against the agreed quality criteria.

Deliverables managed

  • Domain tracking dashboard in Looker Studio
  • Updated pricing and proposal sheets
  • Standardized SOP templates
  • Reusable project management template library

Outcome

All milestones were delivered and approved. The template library continues to support onboarding and operational consistency across the agency.
