The challenge

Kingdom Branding had begun documenting how its work flows, but the processes were not yet finalized or standardized. New project managers and employees joined without a clear picture of how everything operates or what to expect, and each had to work it out for themselves.

The initiative set out to finish that work. The success criteria were that everything was standardized and that onboarding and operational documents were ready for every new project manager and employee.

My role

  • Led the initiative end to end as Program Manager, from scope and success criteria through completion.
  • Wrote the process documentation and templates for pricing, client onboarding across industries, team onboarding, and domain and hosting management.
  • Coordinated three project managers on the work and tracked progress in a structured tracker.
  • Presented the completed library to the CEO for review at the end of the project.

Approach

  1. InitiateOpened with a project charter defining scope, objectives, and success criteria.
  2. PlanBuilt a work breakdown structure for the major deliverable areas, supported by a stakeholder register, risk register, and assumptions log.
  3. DocumentCreated a process guide for each area, with multiple templates attached to each one so teams can use them directly.
  4. Execute and trackDivided the work across the project managers, tracked it in a structured tracker, and kept every document organized in one indexed library. Change control stayed light, since the work was internal.
  5. Review and hand overPresented the finished library to the CEO and made it the reference for onboarding new hires.

Deliverables managed

  • Pricing process documentation, plus updated pricing and proposal sheets
  • Client onboarding process guides, including onboarding for different industries
  • Team onboarding process guide for new project managers and employees
  • Domain and hosting management process, including research steps
  • Domain and hosting tracker with a Looker Studio dashboard covering 100+ domains: expiry and renewal dates, registrar, and whether each domain is registered through Kingdom Branding or independently by the client
  • Standardized SOP and project management templates attached to each process

Process library

Whenever the work surfaced a process or tracker that did not exist yet, I created it. The library now includes:

  • Process guides: Sales, Website Development, Branding, Social Media Marketing, Video, Book Production, Google Analytics and Tag Manager setup, Meta and Google Ads, a RingCentral script, and a Mailchimp tutorial
  • Project workflows: website development, social media, book production, brand suite, and digital ads
  • Trackers and sheets: pricing sheet, proposal process tracker, domain and hosting process sheet and tracker, and website maintenance report list

Outcome

The initiative finished on time. New project managers and employees now have a single reference for how Kingdom Branding operates, instead of starting from a blank page, and the attached templates have helped the team a great deal.

The domain dashboard keeps renewals visible, so nothing goes unchecked.

Lesson learned

Standardization pays off most at onboarding. One organized, indexed library with consistent templates lets new team members contribute faster, and it is far easier to maintain than scattered documents.
