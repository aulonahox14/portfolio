The challenge

Children’s Research Triangle engaged Kingdom Branding to produce a fundraising gala video and supporting campaign assets to drive attendance, donations, and awareness around its annual event. The work spanned in-person filming, editing, and graphics for social media and event communications.

My role

  • Developed and presented the proposal, aligning client stakeholders on scope, objectives, and expected deliverables.
  • Facilitated planning sessions to set timelines, define roles, and structure the production schedule.
  • Coordinated all in-person video sessions, including content structure, scripting discussions, and filming logistics.
  • Led coordination of graphic asset creation for social media and event communications.

Approach

  1. Propose and alignPresented the proposal and aligned stakeholders on scope and deliverables.
  2. PlanRan planning sessions with the client and internal team to establish timelines and roles.
  3. ProduceCoordinated each filming session so it served the campaign narrative.
  4. Post-produceManaged transfer of large media files to the editing team and oversaw post-production review cycles.
  5. ReviewRan a formal review and revision process on every deliverable before client submission.

Deliverables managed

  • Professionally produced gala video
  • Complete set of campaign assets for social media and event communications

Outcome

The project was delivered on schedule with strong client satisfaction, supporting the event’s fundraising goals.
